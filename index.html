A Letter For You

Some feelings are easier to write than to say.

Dear Shikha,

Happy Birthday, my love. ❤️🥹

First of all, I want you to know how special you are to me. Today is the day someone as beautiful and precious as you came into this world, and honestly, I'm so grateful that our paths crossed and I got the chance to love you.

Baby, I know I'm not always the perfect boyfriend. Sometimes I overthink, sometimes I don't understand your feelings the way I should, and sometimes I end up hurting you even when that's the last thing I want to do. But please know that my love for you is genuine. I'm still learning how to love you better, understand you deeper, and become someone who makes you feel safe, valued, and loved. You deserve that kind of love. 🫶🏻

You have a special place in my heart that nobody else could ever take. Your smile, your little talks, your random messages, your cute reactions, and even the smallest things about you mean more to me than you probably realize. Sometimes I catch myself smiling just by thinking about you. It's crazy how one person can make an ordinary day feel so special just by existing. And for me, that person is you. 💗

Even though I haven't seen you in person yet or heard your voice, you have still managed to become such an important part of my life. It makes me excited to think about the day when I finally get to see you, hold your hand, look into your eyes, and tell you how much you mean to me without needing a single text message. Until that day comes, I'll keep cherishing every little moment we share. 🥹❤️

On your birthday, I wish you endless happiness, good health, success, peace, and all the beautiful things your heart desires. I hope you achieve every dream you're working towards. I hope you smile more, worry less, and always remember how precious you are. And whenever life gets difficult, I hope you never forget that you deserve kindness, patience, and happiness.

And baby, I don't just want to be someone who wishes you on your birthday. I want to be someone who celebrates your little achievements, comforts you on your bad days, listens when you need to talk, laughs with you over silly things, and stands beside you while you grow into the person you dream of becoming.

I can't promise that every day will be perfect, but I want to keep trying, keep learning, and keep choosing us with honesty and care. I want us to build something beautiful, something where we both feel understood, respected, and loved.

Happy Birthday once again, my Shikha. 🎂💋❤️

If I could give you one gift today, it would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes, even for a moment. Maybe then you'd understand why you're so special to me and why your happiness means so much to my heart.

I hope this new chapter of your life brings you a thousand reasons to smile. And I hope that, in some small way, I get to be one of those reasons.

I love you, my birthday girl. Today, tomorrow, and in all the little moments yet to come. ❤️🫂

Yours,
Subham ❤️
